Before you redeem

  • Is the offer still live (not expired)?
  • Does the merchant match where you will actually pay?
  • Is the discount a percent, BOGO, or a free item?

Quick glossary

Cashback
A percent of spend returned as statement credit or wallet cash. Only counts on eligible categories.
APR
Yearly interest if you do not pay the credit-card statement in full.
EMI
Splitting a purchase into monthly installments. 0% EMI is useful; interest-bearing EMI is a loan.
BOGO
Buy one get one. For ranking we treat it as about 50% of that spend — a demo estimate, not a bank rule.
Annual fee
What the bank charges each year to keep the card. Subtract this when you compare “high cashback” cards.
Reward points
Points per BDT spent. Convert to cash value before you chase them; they often expire.
